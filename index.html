Why Dagvik

Everything useful.
Nothing shouting.

Dagvik gives your new tab a job: help you see what matters, take the next step and get on with your day.

01

Start with the essentials

Time, search, tasks, weather and quick links are ready on the first screen. Or start from an empty screen and add only what you need.

02

Shape the space around you

Create as many screens as you like for home, work or study. Add widgets — even several of the same kind — then move and resize them on a calm 40 px grid.

03

Protect your attention

Focus mode, Pomodoro and site blocking make room for deep work. Task reminders appear in a small window with a short sound, without system notifications.

Private by design

Your workspace
stays yours.

Dagvik works without an account and keeps working offline. When you are online, your workspace is synchronized with the Dagvik server under an anonymous internal identity — no email, no password, no profile. Browsing history and your own background images never leave your device.
