Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Dagvik is a Chrome new-tab extension. It works without an account and keeps working without a network connection. This policy explains what the extension stores, what it sends to the Dagvik server and to other services, how long data is kept and how to delete it.

In shortYour workspace is stored in your browser and synchronized automatically with the Dagvik server under an anonymous internal identity — no account, email or password. Browsing history and your own background images never leave your device. Synchronized data is kept until you delete it. Dagvik does not sell data, show ads or send activity analytics.

1. Who is responsible

Controller: [legal name and postal address of the operator]. Privacy contact: the Feedback form in Dagvik Settings, or [privacy email]. A request sent from the extension is linked to your installation, so we can find the data it refers to.

2. Data stored on your device

Screens, settings, widget layouts and widget settings (including notes and day-planner entries), tasks and their reminders, quick links, blocked and allowed domains and Pomodoro settings are stored in Chrome local storage, together with timestamps of when each item was last changed or deleted. Your own PNG, JPEG and WebP backgrounds are stored in the browser's IndexedDB. Diagnostics (at most 100 entries for seven days), reminder delivery receipts, running timers, cached weather, browser-history suggestions and the suggestions you hid stay on the device only.

3. Synchronization with the Dagvik server

On first start the extension creates an anonymous internal identity on the Dagvik server. There is no sign-in, registration or email. Whenever you are online, your workspace is synchronized automatically: changes made offline are uploaded when the connection returns, and the most recent change of each item wins.

The server copy contains your screens, settings, layouts and widget settings (including note and day-planner text and the selected weather city or approximate weather coordinates), tasks with their text and reminders, quick links, blocked and allowed domains, Pomodoro settings, change timestamps and deletion markers. A deletion marker holds only an item's identifier and deletion time, not its content. The server copy never contains browsing history, your background images, diagnostics or running timers. Each identity can store at most 25 MB. Server rows are protected so that one identity cannot read another identity's data.

4. Feedback

The Feedback form sends the name, email address and message you enter, your interface language and the extension version to the Dagvik server, linked to your installation's identity. Each installation can send up to five messages per hour. Feedback is only sent when you submit the form.

5. Weather

For current-location weather your public IP address is sent to ipwho.is to estimate a city; the result is kept on your device for 12 hours. Forecast coordinates are rounded to a cell of about 11 km and only that cell is sent to the Dagvik weather service, which shares one cached forecast per cell with everyone in it and refreshes it from Open-Meteo at most once an hour. Cached forecasts carry no user identifier and are removed after two days. To protect the service, requests to Open-Meteo are counted per installation (or per one-way hash of the IP address) for one hour; the counters hold no location and are removed after one day. If the service is unavailable, the extension asks Open-Meteo directly. City names you search for are sent to the Open-Meteo geocoding service.

6. Other network requests

While autocomplete is enabled, Google receives the partial text you type into the search widget; a submitted search goes to the selected search provider. For HTTP links the favicon may be requested from Google's favicon service with the site address; for HTTPS links the browser requests the site's own icon. The optional History permission is requested only when you ask for quick-link suggestions; up to 500 recent entries are read on your device and never sent to Dagvik. Task reminders open a small Dagvik window with a short sound and do not use system notifications.

7. How long data is kept and how to delete it

Synchronized workspace data is kept without a time limit until you delete it, also after the extension is uninstalled. Deleting an item in Dagvik removes its content from the server at the next synchronization; only the deletion marker remains. To delete everything stored on the server, send a deletion request through the Feedback form before uninstalling the extension — the request identifies your installation, and we delete all of its data. Feedback is kept until you ask for its deletion. Data stored only on your device is removed when you uninstall Dagvik or clear its storage in Chrome.

8. Legal basis, service providers and your rights

Synchronization is part of the service you install (performance of a contract, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Abuse limits, request counters and the shared weather cache rely on our legitimate interest in a secure and available service (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Feedback is processed to answer your request. The server copy, feedback and weather cache are hosted by Supabase as our processor in the European Union (Ireland); any transfer outside the EEA relies on the Standard Contractual Clauses of the Supabase data processing agreement. You may request access, correction, restriction, objection, portability or deletion through the contact above, and you may lodge a complaint with the data-protection authority of your country.

9. Sharing and selling

Dagvik does not sell personal data, use it for advertising or credit decisions, or handle payment details. Data is shared only with the service providers named above, as needed to provide the feature you use.

10. Changes

This policy is updated when the extension's data practices change. The date above identifies the current version. Material changes are also disclosed in the extension and its store listing.
