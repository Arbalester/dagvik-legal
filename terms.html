You may use Dagvik for personal and lawful productivity purposes. You are responsible for the links, tasks, notes and other content you enter.

2. Your data on the device and on the server

Dagvik works without registration and keeps working offline. Your workspace is stored in your browser profile and, when you are online, synchronized automatically with the Dagvik server under an anonymous internal identity, as described in the Privacy Policy. Clearing Chrome extension data or uninstalling Dagvik removes the copy on your device; there is no sign-in to restore the server copy in another browser.
